# Disclaimer

Last updated: 21 October 2026

## Not investment advice

Disclosly shows information from company disclosures and calculations made from that information, with their sources. It is not investment advice. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security, and it gives no price targets or portfolio advice.

Disclosly is not a licensed investment adviser. For investment decisions, talk to a licensed professional.

## Accuracy

Answers are written by language models from public disclosures on KAP and SEC EDGAR. Every cited figure is checked against its source, but answers can still be incomplete or wrong. The source documents are the authority: open the source of any figure before you use it.

## Coverage

Disclosly covers a limited set of companies and periods. When it finds no evidence, it says so instead of guessing.
