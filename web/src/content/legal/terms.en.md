# Terms of use

Last updated: 21 October 2026

By signing in to Disclosly you accept these terms.

## The service

Disclosly answers questions about companies using their public disclosures on KAP and SEC EDGAR, and shows the source of every figure. It is in closed beta: only invited addresses can sign in, and access can end at any time.

## Not investment advice

Disclosly gives information, not investment advice. It does not tell you to buy, sell or hold any security, and it gives no price targets or portfolio advice. See the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).

## Answers can be wrong

Answers are written by language models. We check that every cited figure appears in its source, but an answer can still miss context, use an outdated document or misread a table. Check the source before you rely on a figure.

## Fair use

- One account per person. Do not share your account.
- There is a daily question limit and a per-minute rate limit.
- Do not try to get around the limits, the bot check or the sign-in.
- Do not use Disclosly to collect data in bulk or to attack the service.

We can suspend an account that breaks these rules.
